How we work

Clear Complicated things still have to be readable.
Grounded We stay with real data and do not tell more than the evidence carries.
Energetic Insight should not end at looking; it should end at doing.
Our evidence standard

Every figure on this site names its source and its date. Every dataset states its scope and what to know before you use it. We do not present as real what we cannot yet show is real — that is how the whole system works.

Landometer today is a city data and AI ecosystem for decisions about real places: land, location, city life, and what gets done locally.
